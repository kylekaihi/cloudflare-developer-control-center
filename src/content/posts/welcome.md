The Developer Control Center combines a Cloudflare Pages dashboard, a Worker API, D1 history, Access authentication, Cloudflare One Mesh, and lightweight VPS agents.

## Operations dashboard

Open the **Dashboard** navigation item to view node health, service status, historical metrics, incidents, and allowlisted operations.
